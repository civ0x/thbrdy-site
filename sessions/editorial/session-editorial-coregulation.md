## Specific Edits

### AI Tic Removal

1. **Opening "You've felt this before."** — Hollow intimacy play / essay template opener. The author's voice is more declarative. Replace the opening frame: lead with the phenomenon and the science, not the reader's imagined experience. Something like: "Co-regulation is one of the oldest capacities mammals have — the ability to use another nervous system as a regulatory resource."

2. **"The behavioral phenomenon is robust across dozens of studies and diverse dyadic contexts."** — Generic academic hedging. Lead with the distinction: what's replicated (behavior) vs. what's unsettled (mechanism). Don't hedge the frame.

3. **"I want to be precise about where the evidence is strong, where it's suggestive, and where it doesn't exist yet."** — Defensive. Asks permission to be rigorous. Replace: "Here's what's established, what's promising, and what's missing." Or better, just let the three-tier structure speak for itself without announcing it.

4. **"remarkably well-suited"** — "Remarkably" is soft qualifier. "Well-suited" is sufficient.

5. **"Genuine but Early-Stage"** heading or equivalent — "Genuine" is doing soft-sale work. The evidence *is* solid; label the section for what it does: "The Evidence" or "What the Research Shows."

### Factual Updates

1. **CMS digital mental health treatment codes "(effective January 2025)"** — This phrasing sounds future-facing but these codes are now 14+ months in effect. Update: "introduced in January 2025" or "now in place for over a year."

2. **"Phase 0 begins in February 2026"** — This is now in the past. Update to past tense: "Phase 0 began in late February 2026" or specify current status.

3. **"Notice's beta just shipped"** — If the beta shipped more than 4 weeks before the essay date, "just shipped" is soft. Consider: "Notice's beta shipped to the Jhourney community in [month]."

### Voice Consistency

The essay finds its voice by Section 04 ("Enthusiasm doesn't get to override evidence") and maintains it through the open questions and the bet. The opening (lines 15–39) is the outlier — softer, more essay-template. Tighten the opening to match the closing voice.

Specific voice fixes:
- Section 05 heading "Open Questions" is weaker than the subtitle "What I Don't Know and What Worries Me." Use the stronger framing as the heading.
- "as a felt presence" is redundant with "Something your body can entrain to." Pick one image.
